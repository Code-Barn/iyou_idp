## Article I: The Four Immutable Mission Invariants (The PPT Lock-In)

These four rules govern all 19 ecosystem satellites today at the code level and become irrevocable legal covenants upon execution of the Perpetual Purpose Trust (PPT) deed:

1. **The 0.00% Protocol Take-Rate & Zero-Custody Invariant:**  
   No `iyou_` protocol, relay, or satellite application may ever skim a percentage fee from merchant sales, peer-to-peer labor, rides, or creator tips, nor hold user funds in escrow. All commercial commerce settles directly between buyer and seller (Square Direct, Stripe Direct, Lightning/Cashu, or cash).
2. **The Free Single-Operator & Consumer Baseline ($0 Forever):**  
   Individual citizens (`Sovereign-Edge` / `Managed-Basic`) and single-location independent operators (e.g., an independent neighborhood restaurant on `iyou_dine` or solo journalist on `iyou_news`) retain full access to core operational workflows—storefront, KDS, table QR, POS sync, and Nostr/Blossom publishing—at **$0/month** with zero artificial feature crippling.
3. **The Ad-Free Sovereign Invariant & Zero-Surveillance Ads:**  
   Sovereign accounts authenticating with client-side keys (`did:key`, Tiers 2 & 3) are structurally immune to advertising (`show_ads = False`). Custodial accounts (`did:web`, Tier 1 `Managed-Basic`) may render non-tracking, contextual HTML placements (`_contextual_ad.html`) strictly prohibited from loading third-party ad networks, tracking pixels, or behavioral cookies.
4. **Copyleft Core & Poison-Pill Asset Defense:**  
   Core protocols, cryptographic specs, apex domains (`iyou.me`), trademarks (`iyou_`), and canonical repositories (AGPLv3/GPLv3) vest in an irrevocable Perpetual Purpose Trust with zero equity shareholders. Any attempt to convert core protocols into extractive tolls triggers automatic asset surrender to the Electronic Frontier Foundation (EFF) or Software Freedom Conservancy (SFC).

## Article III: The Pre-PPT Bootstrap Bridge (From Today to Lock-In)

Because forming the Perpetual Purpose Trust and Commercial Operating LLC requires legal filing capital and clean IP assignment, execution follows three gated phases:

### Phase A: Code-Level Enforcement & Cash-Flow Seeding (Now — Pre-Entity)
* **Ship Latent Pro Primitives First:** Build the organizational and tier fields (`parent_org`, `license_tier`, `monetization_tier`, `cdn_acceleration`) into `iyou_idp` and `iyou_dine` now so Pro accounts can be activated immediately without a schema rewrite.
* **Personal Income + Voluntary Setup Bridge:** Use new day-job income to cover initial Stage 1 Hetzner bare-metal (`$185/mo`) while offering voluntary, flat-fee white-glove menu/POS onboarding (`$100–$200` via the local Curator guild) to early restaurants that want hands-on setup rather than DIY onboarding.
* **Pro-Tier Break-Even Targets (Zero Donations Required):**
  * **3 Pro Restaurant Groups (`$79/mo`):** Generates **$237/mo**, covering 100% of Stage 1 Hetzner infrastructure (`$185/mo`).
  * **27 Pro Restaurant Groups (`$79/mo`):** Generates **$2,133/mo**, covering 100% of Stage 2 bare-metal infrastructure (`$2,103/mo` for 9,000 kitchens).
  * **439 Pro Restaurant Groups (`$79/mo`) — or 200 Pro Groups + 4 Municipal `iyou_node` SLAs:** Generates **$34,681/mo**, funding the entire Stage 2 budget including 3 full-time SRE/support salaries (`$34,603/mo`).

### Phase B: Dual-Entity Formation & Asset Assignment (The Lock-In Event)
* **Incorporate Commercial Operating LLC:** Establish the operating entity to hold Stripe/invoicing accounts, sign Pro/Enterprise SLAs, sell physical hardware/prints, and shield personal assets.
* **Execute Perpetual Purpose Trust (PPT) Deed:** Transfer `iyou.me`, `namecharts.org`, trademarks (`iyou_`), root DNSSEC/TLS keys, and canonical repositories into the irrevocable PPT with EFF/SFC poison-pill covenants.
* **Grant Irrevocable Public License:** The PPT grants the open public (and the Commercial LLC) an irrevocable AGPLv3/CC0 license to build and sell commercial services on top of the commons, subject to Article I invariants.

### Phase C: Treasury Surplus & Community Reinvestment
* **Surplus Waterfall:** Once Commercial LLC revenue exceeds infrastructure, SRE payroll, and founder family security reserves, net surplus flows upward via sponsorship grants to the PPT and local community initiatives (`iyou_coop`, `iyou_dona`, and local headquarters jobs).
